# Loleza Ridge — Reference UI redesign

The second supplied hotel reference was selected as the visual direction because it has the strongest balance of premium hospitality, clear booking conversion, editorial typography, service cards, featured accommodation and warm neutral space.

## Applied
- Warm ivory / espresso / champagne-gold palette.
- Transparent hero navigation that becomes a clean ivory navbar on scroll.
- Strong mobile menu with opaque ivory panel and dark readable controls.
- Large editorial hero typography with controlled dark image overlay for contrast.
- Floating booking/search card immediately below the hero.
- Services section with premium icon cards.
- Featured lodge/about split with overlapping photography.
- Accommodation cards with stronger visual hierarchy and direct booking CTA.
- Experience cards with photography and hover motion.
- Warm offer/testimonial treatments and consistent footer styling.
- Existing legal routes remain separate from the landing page.
- Existing booking modal, WhatsApp CTA, gallery lightbox and responsive behavior are retained.

The supplied screenshots are used as design references only; they are not inserted into the website as hotel photography.
